About the game

Side Hustle City is a choice-driven survival sim about the gig economy. You piece together a month of income from a classifieds-style job board: moving furniture, walking a husky with four tangled leashes, tutoring math, running a flea market stall, driving delivery for an app that pays less when you decline orders.

Every job is a short hands-on challenge built around a real skill, from lifting with your knees to reading a client's face to checking whether a delivery order beats a dollar a mile after gas. Rent is due every seven days. Stress makes your hands shake. Clients remember how you treated them.

The job board is an app called Hustl, and it works on you the way real gig apps work on their drivers. It picks a job for you every day, pays a bonus for a three-day streak, drops your priority when you choose your own work, puts surge pay on the worst-paying job, and sets a weekly quest that pays all or nothing. Some of it is worth chasing. The game never tells you which.

Jobs you do well make you better at them: more pay, then specialist work that asks for you by name. Pick how tight the month is before you start, from Steady to Brutal, where jobs pay less, rent is higher and a surprise cost lands every week with three ways out and none of them free.

At the end of the month, the game shows you the math: paid hours, lost pay, what the app's offers really paid per hour next to the jobs you chose yourself, who the month made you, and what you learned to carry into your next run.
